## Plain-English summary

Many owners hear “eat less, walk more.” Useful — incomplete. A 2026 paper in *BMC Veterinary Research* examined behavioural susceptibility to environmental influences in obesity using a companion-animal (dog) model. The practical message for pet parents: household environment and behaviour patterns are part of the story. PAWai’s role is to help you notice and record those patterns so a veterinarian can interpret them.

## Owner moves that are usually safe to start

- Count extra calories (treats, table scraps, dental chews)
- Keep short weekly notes on activity and appetite
- Take a consistent side-view photo for body-condition comparison
- Ask your vet for a body-condition score instead of guessing from breed stereotypes

## What not to do from a social post

- Do not start extreme fasting or unvetted “detox” diets
- Do not stop prescribed food without veterinary guidance
- Do not assume a skinny look equals metabolic health
